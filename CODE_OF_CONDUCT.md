# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple standard: be respectful, be constructive, and
assume good faith.

- Disagreement about code, design, or the ontology's contents is normal and
  welcome — keep it focused on the work.
- Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language or behavior are
  not tolerated, in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project
  space.
- Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that
  don't meet this standard, and may block repeat offenders.

To report a concern, email the maintainer at ecgenius.life@gmail.com. Reports
are handled privately and in confidence.

This is a short, project-scale code of conduct rather than an adoption of a
longer external template — if the project grows a larger contributor base,
adopting the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) in
full is a reasonable next step.
